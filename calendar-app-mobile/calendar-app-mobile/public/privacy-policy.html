Privacy Policy for 365 Days Calendar

Last updated: January 2026

Privacy First: 365 Days Calendar is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

Data Collection

365 Days Calendar does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All notes and calendar entries are stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage).

We do not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your usage
  • Use cookies for tracking
  • Share data with third parties

Data Storage

Your data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers. All calendar entries, notes, and preferences are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.

This means:

  • Your data stays on your device
  • No cloud synchronization
  • Complete control over your information
  • Data is cleared if you clear browser data

Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools. It is a completely standalone Progressive Web App (PWA).

Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Storage: To save your calendar entries and notes locally on your device
  • Notifications: (Optional) To remind you of important dates or notes

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security. We recommend:

  • Using device lock/password protection
  • Keeping your device software updated
  • Being cautious about who has access to your device

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, please contact: support@365dayscalendar.app
